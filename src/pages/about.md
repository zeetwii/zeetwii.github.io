# Career

Most of my career has been in the DoD, doing everything from hacking airplanes and power grids to building drones and counter-drone systems.  Most of that life isn't publically releasable, but I do try to publish a lot of open source and public research when I can.  I also have a lot of fun building things on the side, and I try to share those projects as well.

# Public Work

- DEFCON 27 (2019) : Aerospace Village
  - Bricks in the Air - [Hacking Aircraft via the 1553 data bus](https://github.com/deptofdefense/BricksInTheAir)
    - This was the first workshop I built for DEFCON, and it's become the longest running workshop in the Aerospace Village.  My friends and I came up with the idea after trying to figure out the most unclass way to teach people how to hack aircraft, and what's more unclassified than LEGOs?
- DEFCON 28 (2020) : Aerospace Village
  - CPXSimpleSat - [An introduction to hacking satellites](https://github.com/deptofdefense/CPXSimpleSat)
    - This was a part of the Air Force's Hack-A-Sat competition, and I built this workshop to teach people how to hack satellites via exploiting ground stations in a recreation of one of the earliest public satellite hacks.  We used hardware from Adafruit and made the workshop interactable over Twitch, which was a first for me.  
  - DDSat-1 - [An introduction to hacking satellites over RF](https://github.com/deptofdefense/DDSat)
    - This was a companion to CPXSimpleSat, and it was a workshop that taught people how to hack satellites over RF.  Like CPXSimpleSat, we used hardware from Adafruit and made the workshop interactable over Twitch.  Trying to represent RF messages over a text channel was a challenge, but we made it work.  This workshop was also a part of the Air Force's Hack-A-Sat competition.
- DEFCON 29 (2021) : 
  - Aerospace Village
    - DDRover - [A second attempt at doing RF hacking over Twitch](https://github.com/deptofdefense/DDRover)
      - This was a follow up to DDSat-1, and it was a workshop that taught people how to hack drones over RF.  Like the previous two workshops, we used hardware from Adafruit and made the workshop interactable over Twitch.  This time, we mimiced a mars rover, and we had a lot of fun with it.  We even had people sending dance routines to the rover by the end of the workshop.  
      - Youtube Video of the workshop: [Space Rover Hacking](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FOdV_zSyqiM)
  - Car Hacking Village
    - PKE - [Hacking Car Keys to Track and Identify People](https://github.com/zeetwii/pke)
      - This was a talk I gave to the Car Hacking Village about my dissertation work on hacking car keys to track and identify people.  I also released a tool that allows people to do this themselves, and it has been used by quite a few interested parties to do research and work on this topic.  
      - Youtube Video of the talk: [Vehicle ID and Tracking via Passive Keyless Entry](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b63CCwjYL4I)
- DEFCON 30 (2022) : ICS Village
  - Hack the Microgrid - [Exploiting weather data to hack a microgrid](https://github.com/deptofdefense/HackTheMicrogrid)
    - This was a workshop I built for the ICS Village that taught people how to hack a microgrid by exploiting weather data.  It was based on work we did with the Army and was meant to get people excited about an upcoming bug bounty program for the Army's microgrid.  It was a lot of fun to build, and we had a lot of fun running it.  
    - Youtube Video of the workshop: [Hack the Microgrid](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=odNiFW-2yDo)
- DEFCON 31 (2023) : AI Village
  - LLM Rover - [Chatbot Take The Wheel](https://github.com/zeetwii/llmRover)
    - This was a workshop that I built for the Chief Digital and AI Office within the DoD.  It was a part of the AI Village's first Generative Red Team event, and it was meant to both show how you could use LLMs to control a rover, and also how you could exploit LLMs to affect the rover's behavior.  It was a lot of fun to build, and we had a lot of fun running it.
- DEFCON 32 (2024) : AI Village
  - Combee - [A multi-model Rover](https://github.com/zeetwii/combee)
    - This was a workshop I built to show off both how to use different models together to control a rover, and also how exploits of those individual models cascade and affect the rover's behavior.  It was a lot of fun to build, and we had a lot of fun running it.  We also had a lot of fun with the rover itself, and Combee went on to go to several other conferences and events.
    - News Article about Combee: [AI-powered Combee robot shows how voice commands can transform defense operations](https://wjla.com/news/local/ai-powered-combee-robot-voice-commands-transform-department-of-defense-operations-competitive-studies-project-dc-nick-ashworth-boring-desk-job-recruitment-government-robotics-tech)
- DEFCON 33 (2025) : AI Village
  - Meshtastic Demos - [Using local models to make Meshtastic devices smarter](https://github.com/zeetwii/meshtasticDemos)
    - This year, DEFCON was asking all of the villages to try and do something interesting with Meshtastic to show off the capabilities of the network and inspire people to try it.  I built a few demos that used local models to make Meshtastic devices smarter and more accessible.  They focused on showing how local models pair well with the text limitations of the Meshtastic network, and how they can be used to make it easier for people to discover and use devices on the network.  
- DEFCON 34 (2026) : AI Village
  - AI Plays Pokemon - [Using harnesses and tools to make local models more capable](https://github.com/zeetwii/aiPlaysPokemon)
    - 2026 had a lot of stuff going on with AI agents, and there were a lot of policy discussions about how to regulate them, with many people overly focusing on the sizes of the models as the main problem.  I built a workshop that showed how you could use harnesses and tools to make local models more capable, and have them compete and accomplish tasks that faster and more efficently than larger models.  We ended up talking to a lot of government officals about what this meant for policy regulations, and what it means to take a more holistic approach to regulating AI agents.  
    - Youtube Video of the workshop: [AI Plays Pokemon](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Egzf_aWSxZc)
